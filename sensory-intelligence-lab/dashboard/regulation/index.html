How the soundscapes work


Each soundscape is generated in real time rather than played from a recording. The system continuously shapes elements like rhythm, harmony, texture, and movement to support a gradual shift in attention and nervous system state. Because the sounds are created generatively, no two sessions are ever exactly the same — even when you select the same option.


The soundscapes follow a principle used in music therapy called the iso principle. Instead of forcing the listener into a new state immediately, the music begins by matching your current energy, then slowly guides you toward the desired state. This gradual transition helps the nervous system adapt comfortably and naturally.


The structure and musical elements of each soundscape are guided by music cognition research and the neuroscience of sound perception, which explore how people respond to sound and how specific musical features can support attention, emotional regulation, and a sense of stability.
